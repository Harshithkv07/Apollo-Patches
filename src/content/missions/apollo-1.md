Apollo 1 was meant to be the first crewed Apollo mission, scheduled to launch on 21 February 1967. On 27 January, during a preflight test on the launch pad at Cape Kennedy, a fire swept through the command module and killed Gus Grissom, Ed White and Roger Chaffee.

At the time the mission was designated AS-204. In the spring of 1967, NASA announced that the flight planned for Grissom, White and Chaffee would be known as Apollo 1. The crew's patch, designed in 1966 by Allen Stevens of North American Rockwell, already carried that name.

NASA describes it simply: an Apollo spacecraft in Earth orbit, with the stars and stripes of the U.S. flag in the background.
