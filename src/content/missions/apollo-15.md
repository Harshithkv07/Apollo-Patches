Apollo 15 was the first mission to the Moon with the Lunar Roving Vehicle. Dave Scott and Jim Irwin landed the lunar module *Falcon* at Hadley–Apennine while Al Worden orbited in the command module *Endeavour*. The mission launched on 26 July 1971 and splashed down on 7 August.

NASA's description of the patch is exact. It is red, white and blue throughout: a narrow band of blue and a narrow band of red around a wider band of white. In the center disc, red, white and blue symbols of flight sit over an artist's view of the Hadley–Apennine landing site. The three stylized birds represent the crew.

According to the National Space Centre, the crew went through hundreds of ideas before asking the Italian fashion designer Emilio Pucci, a former pilot, for help. They turned his square design into a circle, and NASA artist Jerry Elmore finished the art.
