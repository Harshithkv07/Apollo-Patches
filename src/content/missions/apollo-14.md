Apollo 14 launched on 31 January 1971 and returned on 9 February. Alan Shepard and Edgar Mitchell landed the lunar module *Antares* while Stuart Roosa orbited in the command module *Kitty Hawk*.

According to NASA's caption, the crew designed the patch themselves. It shows the astronaut lapel pin approaching the Moon, leaving a comet trail from the liftoff point on Earth.

The pin was the astronaut corps' own badge, adopted a few years earlier. Astronauts who haven't yet flown in space wear it in silver, and those who have wear it in gold.
