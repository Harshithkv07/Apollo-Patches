Apollo 8 was the first crewed launch of the Saturn V, and its crew were the first people to orbit the Moon. Frank Borman, Jim Lovell and Bill Anders launched on 21 December 1968 and splashed down on 27 December.

Lovell sketched the emblem while flying back to Houston. His idea does double duty: a large red 8 for the mission number, and a figure 8 tracing the path out to the Moon and back. A NASA artist polished the drawing, and it became the official patch.

The outline carries a meaning too. According to NASA, the patch's shape stands for the Apollo command module, the capsule the crew flew around the Moon.
