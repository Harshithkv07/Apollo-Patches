Apollo 12 was the second lunar landing. Pete Conrad, Dick Gordon and Al Bean launched on 14 November 1969. Conrad and Bean landed the lunar module *Intrepid* in the Ocean of Storms while Gordon orbited in the command module *Yankee Clipper*. They splashed down on 24 November.

All three were Navy men, and the patch says so twice. NASA's caption says the clipper ship signifies that the crew is all Navy. It also ties the two eras together: clipper ships brought foreign shores closer to the United States, and spacecraft opened the way to the other planets. According to NASA, the blue and gold signal the Navy too.

The four stars honor one more astronaut. There are three for Conrad, Gordon and Bean, and a fourth for C. C. Williams, who was killed in October 1967 when his T-38 crashed on the way to visit his parents.
