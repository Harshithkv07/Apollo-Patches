Apollo 16 launched on 16 April 1972 and splashed down on 27 April. John Young and Charlie Duke landed the lunar module *Orion* while Ken Mattingly orbited in the command module *Casper*. Mattingly was the astronaut pulled from [Apollo 13](/apollo-13) days before launch two years earlier.

NASA's caption describes the patch closely. An eagle perches on a red, white and blue shield over a lunar scene, inside a blue circle of 16 stars, and the crew's surnames complete the bottom of the circle. A gold symbol of flight, outlined in blue, crosses the shield, similar to the one on NASA's seal and insignia.

A NASA artist created the design from ideas submitted by the three crew members.
