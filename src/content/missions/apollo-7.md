Apollo 7 was the first crewed flight of the Apollo program: an 11-day engineering test of the command and service module in Earth orbit. Wally Schirra, Donn Eisele and Walt Cunningham launched on a Saturn IB on 11 October 1968 and splashed down on 22 October.

The emblem shows exactly that. In NASA's description, a command and service module circles the globe trailing an ellipse of orange flame, marking the Earth-orbital nature of the mission. The navy background stands for the depth of space.

According to collectSPACE, Allen Stevens of North American Rockwell submitted designs, and the crew settled on a final version that NASA officials then approved.
